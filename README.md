# UK Housing Market — Power BI Dashboard

Interactive Power BI dashboard visualising UK housing market trends (2023-2025)
across Greater London, Greater Manchester, West Midlands, and West Yorkshire.

Built on top of the [SQL analysis](https://github.com/DanPrangate/uk-housing-sql-analysis)
in the companion repo.

## Dashboard Includes
- Regional average price trends over time
- Price breakdown by property type and county
- New-build vs. established property price comparison
- Top 10 fastest-growing districts by % price change

## Key Insight
While Greater London prices cooled slightly, regional cities across Greater
Manchester, West Yorkshire, and the West Midlands saw the strongest growth —
none of the top 10 growth districts were in London.
